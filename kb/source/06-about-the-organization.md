## Mission & Values

CURE SYNGAP1 (FKA SynGAP Research Fund) is a 501(c)(3) public charity incorporated in 2018. The mission is to improve the quality of life of *SYNGAP1* patients through the research and development of treatments, therapies and support systems. Completely family led, [CURE SYNGAP1 is a leading funder of *SYNGAP1* research](https://curesyngap1.org/resources/grants/). CURE SYNGAP1's mantra is *Collaboration, Transparency & Urgency*. CURE SYNGAP1 is a member of [COMBINEDBrain](https://www.combinedbrain.org/), [The Research Acceleration and Innovation Network (TRAIN)](https://milkeninstitute.org/centers/fastercures/building-nonprofit-capacity/train), the [Personalized Medicine Coalition](http://www.personalizedmedicinecoalition.org/), the [Global Genes Global Advocacy Alliance](https://globalgenes.org/global-advocacy-alliance/), the [Everylife Foundation Community Congress](https://everylifefoundation.org/), & the [Innovation and Value Initiative.](https://www.thevalueinitiative.org/)

CURE SYNGAP1 is committed to serving all members of the community regardless of age, race, color, religion, creed, citizenship status, marital status, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, genetic information, national origin, disability, veteran status or any other protected status under applicable law.

## CURE SYNGAP1 – Impact on the Road to Find a Cure!

*Click on the image above to access the complete [2025 Impact Report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZO-1c1qmav95cS3XuGLdQjdXgAel6u-K/view)!*

Every year since its 2018 incorporation, CURE SYNGAP1 (FKA SynGAP Research Fund) has helped advance our [mission](https://curesyngap1.org/mission-and-values/) "to improve the quality of life of *SYNGAP1* patients through the research and development of treatments, therapies and support systems" for those living with [*SYNGAP1*-Related Disorders](https://curesyngap1.org/what-is-syngap1/) (SRD). In our 2025 Impact Report, our second such publication, you will learn more about our organization rebranding, extensive partnerships, accelerating clinical trial readiness, and our new global collaboration – the CURE SYNGAP1 Collective

We are indebted to the more than 170 [volunteers](https://curesyngap1.org/team/) who are responsible for developing [resources](https://curesyngap1.org/syngap1-resources-for-newly-diagnosed-families/) to help families deal with this disease, activating the community to participate in critical [studies](https://curesyngap1.org/resources/studies/) and [trials](https://curesyngap1.org/clinical-trials/), and [advocating](https://curesyngap1.org/blog/tag/advocacy/) for our patient populations for whom we do everything. CURE SYNGAP1's mantra – *Collaboration, Transparency & Urgency* – remains our cornerstone and demonstrates our commitment to creating positive change in the *SYNGAP1* community, which includes our global families, researchers, institutions, and [partners](https://curesyngap1.org/partners/rare-disease/). We invite you to explore these results and join us in continuing this important work.
